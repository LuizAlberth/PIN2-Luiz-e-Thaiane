The file `pilot_rivers.geojson` contains clipped OpenStreetMap river centerlines
for the study area. © OpenStreetMap contributors. Data license: Open Database
License (ODbL 1.0): https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Source: https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map
Refresh: `PYTHONPATH=backend .venv/bin/python scripts/update_rivers.py`

These mapped centerlines are contextual GIS data, not an official river survey
or a measurement of the water extent on any satellite acquisition date.
